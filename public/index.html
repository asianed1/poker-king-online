POKER KING

Published by Crow New Media
Online multiplayer · 2–8 players · learn poker with friends anywhere
— or join a friend's table —
  • Every player gets 5 cards from a freshly shuffled 52-card deck. You see only your cards.
  • Taking turns clockwise, each player gets up to 3 offers: "Do you want a new card? (Y/N)". The new card is dealt face up so everyone sees it.
  • After Y you go straight to choosing which card to replace (arrows/Tab or the mouse, then Enter or click) — or press N/Esc to reject the new card. Either way the offer counts. Saying N to "new card?" means you stand.
  • Showdown: all hands are revealed and the best poker hand wins. Exact ties split the win — suits never break ties.
  • No betting — just learning which hands win. Press H any time for the hand rankings.
  • On your own? Create a table and press Play vs Dealer to play against the computer 🤖 Dealer. Friends can still join — the Dealer steps aside when a second player arrives.
